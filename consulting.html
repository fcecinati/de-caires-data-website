Consulting

Data science and AI consulting

For organisations that suspect their data could do more. We help you decide what is worth building, then build it properly: honest advice, sound methods, and models that survive contact with the real world.

What we help with

Data and AI strategy

An honest assessment of where data science and AI can genuinely help your organisation, and where they cannot. Roadmaps you can act on, not slideware.

Machine learning and modelling

Predictive models, forecasting and statistical analysis built with scientific rigour: validated properly, documented clearly, and explained in plain language.

Data pipelines and automation

Reliable pipelines that clean, join and prepare your data automatically, so analysis stops being a manual chore and starts being repeatable.

AI adoption and training

Help introducing AI tools into your team's daily work safely and productively, including hands-on training tailored to your context.

How an engagement works

  1. Scoping conversation

    A free call to understand your problem, your data and what success would look like.

  2. Proposal

    A short written proposal with a clear scope, timeline and price. No surprises later.

  3. Delivery

    Regular check-ins as the work progresses, with results shared early and often.

  4. Handover and support

    Documentation, training and a handover your team can actually use, with support available afterwards.
