About

The person behind Opotus

Opotus is a data science and AI studio with one guiding idea: advanced methods are only useful when someone takes the time to apply them properly to your specific problem.

Francesca De Caires

Francesca is a data scientist whose career spans academic research and industry practice. Her peer-reviewed work on data and uncertainty in the water sector has been published in international journals, and she has spent years turning research-grade methods into answers organisations can act on.

Opotus brings that same rigour to a wider range of problems: AI strategy and machine learning for organisations of any sector, specialist analytics for water, and AI-enabled websites and marketing for small businesses. Different audiences, one standard of care.

Francesca works in English and Italian, and every Opotus project, whatever its size, gets her direct attention.

Credentials at a glance

  • PhD-level research background in water sector data science
  • Peer-reviewed publications in international journals
  • Years of applied experience across research and industry
  • Bilingual: English and Italian
